Epigenetic, not genetic.

Your genetic code stays the same all your life. Epigenetic marks don’t. They shift with age and with how you sleep, move, eat and handle stress, which is why your result can change when you do.

Senva reads a small set of these marks. We don’t analyse your genetic code.

Our own model, developed at Umeå University.

Built for saliva

Trained on saliva, the sample you can give at home.

A few key sites

It reads a compact set of epigenetically relevant sites, which keeps the test affordable.

Not a black box

It shows how sites work together in ageing, so we can explain your result.

Linked to your health

It shows how individual sites relate to health, so your report can point to what matters.

Questions.

Is Senva a medical test?

No. Senva is a wellness test that helps you understand your health and what to work on. It doesn’t diagnose disease. If you have health concerns, talk to your doctor.

Do you read my genes?

No. We measure epigenetic marks at a small set of sites. We don’t analyse your genetic code.

Where is my sample analysed?

In a European lab, and your data is handled under the EU’s data protection rules (GDPR).

When can I order?

We plan to launch in the EU in 2027. Join the waiting list and we’ll tell you first.
