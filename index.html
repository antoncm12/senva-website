Know your health. Take charge. Stay healthy for longer.

Senva is a saliva test you take at home. It reads epigenetic marks, the small chemical tags on your DNA that change with how you live, to estimate your biological age and give you clear next steps.

Epigenetic, not genetic.

Your genetic code stays the same all your life. Epigenetic marks don’t. They shift with age and with how you sleep, move, eat and handle stress, which is why your result can change when you do.

Marks on your DNA

The best-studied mark is DNA methylation, a small chemical tag on the DNA letter C. Identical twins start out with almost the same pattern, and it drifts apart as they age (Fraga et al., 2005).

Clocks that measure ageing

In 2013, researchers showed that methylation at a set of DNA sites predicts a person’s age, in blood and across most tissues of the body (Hannum et al., 2013; Horvath, 2013). Newer clocks also predict health and lifespan (Levine et al., 2018; Lu et al., 2019).

Shaped by how you live

Diet, education and body weight are linked to faster or slower epigenetic ageing (Quach et al., 2017). In a two-year trial, moderate calorie restriction slightly slowed the pace of ageing (Waziry et al., 2023).

Senva reads a small set of these marks. We don’t analyse your genetic code.

Questions.

What is biological age?

It’s an estimate of how old your body seems, based on markers such as epigenetic marks, compared with your age in years. If it’s lower than your age in years, your body shows fewer signs of ageing than expected. How epigenetic clocks work

Is Senva a medical test?

No. Senva is a wellness test that helps you understand your health and what to work on. It doesn’t diagnose disease. If you have health concerns, talk to your doctor.

Do you read my genes?

No. We measure epigenetic marks at a small set of sites. We don’t analyse your genetic code.

Why saliva and not blood?

Saliva is easy to give at home, with no needles, and it carries DNA from your own cells. Our model is built for saliva from the start.

Where is my sample analysed?

In a European lab, and your data is handled under the EU’s data protection rules (GDPR).

When can I order?

We plan to launch in the EU in 2027. Join the waiting list and we’ll tell you first.
