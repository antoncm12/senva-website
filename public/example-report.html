About this report

What it is, and what it isn’t

A well-being service

Senva Health estimates your epigenetic age and shows lifestyle-linked methylation patterns, to help you reflect on and track how you live. It does not diagnose, predict or treat any disease.

Epigenetic, not genetic

We measure methylation, chemical marks that change during life. We don’t read your DNA sequence, so the report says nothing about inherited conditions.

Your data

Your sample is analysed in Europe and your data is handled under the GDPR. You can download or delete your results at any time.
